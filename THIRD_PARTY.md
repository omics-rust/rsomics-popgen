Revisions identify the control-plane routing baseline, not a claim that every historical worktree or unpinned benchmark was accepted. The private implementation ledger retains actual body hashes and dispositions.

Behavior authorities are scikit-allel1.3.13 commit f9e17f71b395695d3d99c58f58c76a06c4ad8e12 (MIT), NumPy1.26.4 (BSD-3-Clause), and the original vcftools0.1.17 release/commit1c53c3c73be141103069965403e655536dda9c87 as a separately licensed GPL upstream oracle. No vcftools C++ source, binary or FFI backend is incorporated. The small private source-shaped pairwise reducer adapts NumPy's real-float 128-element/eight-lane reduction algorithm and preserves its notice below. The synthetic casebook goldens were generated by the actual pinned source workflow, not inferred from historical README claims.
